Harsanyi's move: incomplete information means a player is unsure of another's payoffs, so nobody knows the game. Explain how Nature drawing types from a common prior, each player seeing only his own, turns it into imperfect information about Nature's move. Work Tadelis's entry game with a rational or crazy incumbent: build the two-by-four matrix of type-contingent strategies, show the entrant's payoff 2p minus 1 against "rational accommodates, crazy fights", and find the three equilibria at p = 2/3, then explain why only (Enter, AF) survives the check that each type moves optimally at his own node. Discuss the price of the common prior assumption. Use the correlated prior with cells 1/6 and 1/3 to show Bayes' rule moving a belief, and say why independent types leave posteriors equal to the prior.

Bayesian Nash equilibrium: a strategy maps types to actions, Tadelis Def. 12.3. Explain why the plan must cover types that were not drawn: rivals average over them. State Def. 12.4 and why checking one-action deviations type by type suffices; then the equivalence of the ex ante matrix and the interim check (MWG Prop. 8.E.1). Solve chicken with harsh or lenient parents at R = 8, H = 16, L = 0: show the unique BNE, lenient drives and harsh swerves, confirm it type by type (against that rival plan a lenient driver expects 6, a harsh one minus 2), and how retelling the payoffs flips the prediction.

Continuous actions: solve Gibbons's Cournot with a privately known cost through its three first-order conditions. Explain why the high-cost type produces more and the low-cost type less than under complete information, and why firm 1 caring only about the mean of firm 2's output is special to linear demand.

Continuous types: in Tadelis's study groups, show that any best response is a cutoff, derive the cutoff square root of c over the partner's cutoff, and the unique symmetric BNE c to the one-third, read as free-riding: it sits above the cutoff square root of c of a student working alone. Then purification: Gibbons's Battle of the Sexes with private payoff shocks on zero to x, the cutoff solving c squared plus 3c minus x = 0, and the Opera probability tending to 2/3. Stress Gibbons's point that what matters is not that j randomizes but that i is uncertain about j.

Adverse selection: walk through Tadelis's orange grove, values 10, 20, 30 to the seller and 14, 24, 34 to the buyer. Generalize: K qualities with spread s and premium Delta trade up to quality k only if Delta is at least (k minus 1) s over 2.
