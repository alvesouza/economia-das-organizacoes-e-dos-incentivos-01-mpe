# Provenance — Aula 1: Does Organization Matter? Ford × GM × Toyota

**Thesis.** A firm's performance comes from a coherent system of complementary choices, not from
any single practice; that is why the same workers could be GM's worst and Toyota's best, and why
GM could watch the better system and still not copy it.
