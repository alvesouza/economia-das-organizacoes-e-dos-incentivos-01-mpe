The Lemons Ladder

A seller knows the quality of what she sells; the buyer knows only that each of K qualities is equally likely. Every quality is worth Δ more to the buyer than to the seller, so with full information every unit would trade. With one price for all, only sellers whose value is below the price accept — and the buyer pays for the average of that pool. Move the premium and the spread and watch how far up the ladder trade can climb before the market unravels.

Tadelis (2013) §12.3, printed pp. 258–261, Prop. 12.1 (values 10/20/30 and 14/24/34, prob. 1/3); the K-type, spread-s generalization is this page’s, solved by the same argument.

v1 · lowest seller value

What the worst quality is worth to its owner. It shifts every price, and changes nothing else.

s · quality spread

The gap in seller value between adjacent qualities: how much the seller knows that the buyer does not.

K · number of qualities

Qualities v1, v1+s, …, v1+(K−1)s, each with probability 1/K.

Δ · buyer premium

How much more every quality is worth to the buyer: the gain from trade on each unit.

Model: quality k = 1, …, K with probability 1/K; seller value vk = v1 + (k−1)s; buyer value vk + Δ. The buyer offers one price p; a seller of quality k accepts iff p ≥ vk. Qualities 1..k can trade in a Bayesian Nash equilibrium iff the buyer does not lose on that pool at the price that brings in quality k: mean(v1..vk) + Δ ≥ vk, i.e. Δ ≥ s(k−1)/2 (equality included: the buyer is then indifferent). The equilibrium prices for that set are [vk, mean + Δ]. Buyer’s best offer: the vk maximizing (k/K)(Δ − s(k−1)/2). Gains realized: Δ·k/K per unit offered (full information: Δ). Defaults reproduce Prop. 12.1: only L trades, at any p in [10, 14]; the pool {L, M} is worth 19 < 20 and the whole market 24 < 30 (p. 260). The level v1 never changes the trade set — only the spread relative to the premium does. Δ reaches 105 = 30·7/2 so full trade is reachable at every (s, K). Not exposed: unequal probabilities and a multiplicative premium (MWG Example 13.B.1, p. 442), drawn in figure L07-lemons-unravelling. Notes: Notas/L07/L07-05.
