The Patience Threshold

Two players repeat a Prisoner’s Dilemma forever and both play grim trigger: cooperate until anyone defects, then defect forever. Cheating pays the temptation T once and the punishment P every period after; cooperating pays the reward R every period. Move the four payoffs and the discount factor and watch where cooperation stops being self-enforcing, and which average payoffs the folk theorem says can be supported.

Tadelis (2013) §10.2–10.3, eq. (10.3), pp. 193–200; §10.6, Theorem 10.1, pp. 209–214 · Gibbons (1992) §2.3.B, eq. (2.3.1), pp. 90–92

T · temptation

What a player gets by defecting while the other still cooperates: (F, m) for player 1. The one-period gain is T − R.

R · reward

Each player’s payoff when both cooperate, (M, m). The path grim trigger tries to keep.

P · punishment

The static Nash payoff, (F, f). It is what both get forever once the trigger is pulled; the per-period loss is R − P.

S · sucker

What the cooperator gets when the other defects. It does not enter the grim-trigger threshold; it shapes the feasible set and the alternating path.

δ · discount factor

How much tomorrow counts today; equivalently the probability the relationship goes on for one more period (p. 193).

Expressions: grim trigger is an SPE iff R/(1−δ) ≥ T + δP/(1−δ), i.e. δ ≥ (T−R)/(T−P) (Tadelis (10.3), p. 200, and (10.5), p. 202, with vc = R, vd = T, v* = P; Gibbons (2.3.1), p. 92). Off the path (F, f) forever is a stage Nash equilibrium repeated, so no one-stage deviation pays there (Prop. 10.3, p. 198). The alternating path (F, m), (M, f), … gives player 1 (T + δS)/(1+δ) and player 2 (S + δT)/(1+δ) (pp. 212–213); in its “bad” period a player needs (S + δT)/(1+δ) ≥ P, i.e. δ ≥ (P−S)/(T−P), and in its “good” period (T + δS)/(1+δ) ≥ (1−δ)R + δP (pp. 213–214). Defaults are Tadelis’s matrix: δ* = 1/4 and the alternating path needs δ ≥ 1/2. “Gibbons p. 90” loads Figure 2.3.6 (T, R, P, S) = (5, 4, 1, 0): δ* = 1/4 again. The page requires T > R > P > S and says so when the sliders break the ordering.
