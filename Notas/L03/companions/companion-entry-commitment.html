The Credible Threat Desk

An incumbent would like to scare an entrant off by promising a price war. The promise is a Nash equilibrium and still not believable: once entry has happened, fighting pays less than sharing. A bond that the incumbent forfeits if it accommodates changes its own payoff, and so changes what it will do. Move the payoffs and the bond and watch backward induction decide.

Cabral (2017) §7.2, printed pp. 167–171, Figures 7.7–7.9; the same game is MWG Example 9.B.1, pp. 268–269. Payoffs (entrant, incumbent).

Outcomes without a fight

M · monopoly profit

What the incumbent earns if the entrant stays out (entrant gets 0).

DE · entrant, shared market

The entrant’s payoff when it enters and the incumbent accommodates.

DI · incumbent, shared market

The incumbent’s payoff when it accommodates entry, before any bond.

The price war

FE · entrant, price war

The entrant’s payoff if it enters and is fought. Below 0, a believed fight keeps it out.

FI · incumbent, price war

The incumbent’s payoff from fighting. The threat is credible only if this beats accommodating.

Commitment

B · bond

A penalty the incumbent pays if it accommodates after posting the bond. Writing it is free.

Solution: backward induction (Tadelis Proposition 8.1, p. 153; with no ties it is the unique SPE). After entry the incumbent retaliates iff FI > DI (no bond) or FI > DI − B (bond). The entrant enters iff its post-entry payoff is strictly above 0. The incumbent posts the bond iff that raises its own payoff strictly. Ties: the page reports them and resolves them toward accommodation, entry declined, and no bond; at a tie the game has two pure SPE and the other one is named in the read-out. Defaults reproduce Cabral pp. 168–170: without the bond the SPE is (enter, accommodate) with (10, 20); with B = 40 retaliation pays 10 against −20, the entrant stays out, the incumbent gets 50, and the value of commitment is 50 − 20 = 30. Cabral’s figure prints (−10, 10) after a price war; the text of p. 168 gives the incumbent −10 there; both are below 20 and above −20, so the answer is the same. The MWG preset uses Example 9.B.1 with no bond (B = 0): out (0, 2), fight (−3, −1), accommodate (2, 1). Not exposed: a costly bond (it would subtract from the incumbent’s bond branch).
