\noindent\sloppy These are detailed solutions to the 12 book exercises of the list
\texttt{pratica-lecture-04.md} (Lecture~4: credible threats, subgames, subgame-perfect equilibrium, the
definition of a repeated game; Stackelberg and time-inconsistency exercises), in the list's order from easy to
hard; each heading shows the item's number, level and difficulty from 1 (apply a definition) to 5 (graduate
level). Statements are in the list and are not repeated here. Every solution has the same six parts: (a) what is
asked; (b) the method, with book section, page and lecture slide; (c) the solution, every step shown; (d) the economic
interpretation; (e) the verification, by \texttt{check\_L04.py} in this folder (new items) or the check scripts of
\texttt{lista-reais-lectures-01-05-solutions/} (reused items), and against the book's worked answer where one
exists; (f) common mistakes. Slide numbers in the reused solutions refer to the drafts of the earlier
real-exercise list and may differ by a few slides from the final \texttt{Aula/Lecture04.pdf}. Items~9 and~12
(time inconsistency, Tadelis \S8.3.4) are reading: they are not in the slides of Lecture~4.
