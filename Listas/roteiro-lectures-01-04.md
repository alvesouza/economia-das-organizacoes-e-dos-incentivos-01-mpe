# Study roadmap — real-exercise list, Lectures 1–4

Roadmap for `Listas/lista-reais-lectures-01-04.pdf` (24 real book exercises, 6 per lecture).
It carries the two tables `/study-pack` reads; the pack is `Listas/study-pack-roteiro-lectures-01-04.pdf`.
Reading is limited to the sections the 24 exercises draw on, not the full lecture readings
(those are in `Map/study-guide.md`). Muñoz-Garcia ranges include the worked answer that follows
each exercise. Book names are `Livros/book-registry.json` keys, so each chapter gets its own
page offset. Printed pages throughout.

Order: read the section, then do the block's exercises in list order (foundations → exam →
advanced). Worked solutions: `Listas/lista-reais-lectures-01-04-solutions.pdf`.
