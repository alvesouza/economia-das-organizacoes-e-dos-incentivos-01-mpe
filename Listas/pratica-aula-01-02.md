# Practice plan — Aula 1 (Does Organization Matter?) + Aula 2 (Bargaining)

30 existing book exercises, cited by number and page only, worked in one sequence of rising
complexity: **12 for Aula 1** (the book items of [[Listas/pratica-aula-01|the Aula 1 plan]]) and
**18 for Bargaining**. Sources: Milgrom & Roberts (1992), Cabral (2017), Varian (2014, with
Calculus), MWG (1995). No answers in M&R, Cabral or MWG; Varian's are in its Answers appendix.
Topics: [[rules/01_organizacoes_importam|Topic 1]] · [[rules/02_barganha|Topic 2]] · index:
[[Map/exercises-index|exercises-index]] · class plan: [[Map/study-guide|study guide]].

**Class split.** In the draft schedule Bargaining covers Aulas 2–3: Aula 2 is value maximization
and Coase (block B1); Aula 3 is commitment, alternating offers and private information (B2–B3).
The whole topic is here; if the real Aula 2 stops at Coase, B2–B3 can wait.

**Verification.** Re-read in the converted MD on 2026-10-13: M&R pp. 52–53, 163–165; Cabral
p. 346; Varian pp. 628, 687; MWG pp. 302–303. The rest (M&R pp. 18, 119–120; Cabral pp. 11,
62–64, 117, 180–182) carry `Verified = yes` in the index and in the Aula 1 plan (2026-10-09). One caveat: MWG **9.B.13** reads "9.B.78" in the OCR. It sits between 9.B.12 and
9.B.14, and MWG's own text in Appendix A cites "Exercises 9.B.7 and 9.B.13" for this game, so the
number is safe. MWG is a 2-up scan, so its exercise page is one of the pair 302–303.

## Suggested study order

1. **Aula 1 cases.** Read M&R Ch. 1 (pp. 2–18) and Cabral Ch. 1, then do items **1–3, 11**.
2. **Boundaries.** Read Cabral §3.4–3.5 (pp. 54–59), then do **4, 12, 13**.
3. **Complementarities.** Read M&R Ch. 4 pp. 106–113, then do **5, 14, 15**, then companions C1–C3.
4. **Aula 2 — Coase.** Read M&R Ch. 2 pp. 35–39, Varian §32.3 and §35.2, and Cabral §5.1, then do **6–9, 16, 17**. Do the proofs **26, 27** once 16–17 are solid.
5. **Commitment and alternating offers.** Read M&R Ch. 5 p. 139 ("achieving commitment"), Varian Ch. 30 pp. 572–584 and Cabral §7.2, then do **10, 18, 19, 20**. Do MWG **28** before **29**, as the study guide suggests.
6. **Private information and bargaining position.** Read M&R Ch. 5 pp. 134–139 and 140–149, then do **21, 22, 23**, then **30**.
7. **Rest of the advanced block.** Do **24, 25** last; 25 needs firm-level data, so skip it if none is at hand.
