\noindent\textbf{How to read.} Each item states what is asked, the method (with the book pages),
the solution, a verification note and a boxed answer. In every perfect Bayesian equilibrium (PBE)
the solution gives the strategies, the beliefs on the path (Bayes' rule) and off the path
(chosen to deter deviations, with the range that works), and sequential rationality at every
information set. Messages and replies are written as in the exercise. This unit is
\emph{estimated}: Lecture~8 has no slides yet, so the references are to the books. Tadelis
prints no answers; Mu\~noz-Garcia prints worked answers for items 4, 8, 11, 14 and 23, and each
of those items records that the printed answer agrees. The solutions of MWG 13.C.1, 13.C.2,
13.C.4 and 13.C.5 were compared with the MWG solutions manual; they agree except for 13.C.4, where
the manual's formula drops the lowest type's boundary condition (item 25 explains).
All numbers and equilibrium sets are asserted by \texttt{check\_T1.py}, \texttt{check\_T2.py}
and \texttt{check\_T3.py}.
