\newcommand{\solintro}{\sloppy These are detailed solutions to the 22 real book exercises of
\texttt{Listas/pratica-lecture-02.md}, in the list's order, which runs from easy to hard. Each heading shows the item's
number in the list, its level and its difficulty from 1 (apply a definition) to 5 (graduate level). Statements
are reproduced verbatim in the list and are not repeated here. Every solution has the same parts: (a) what is asked, in one line;
(b) the method, with the book section and page and the lecture slide (printed slide numbers of
\texttt{Aula/Lecture02.pdf}); (c) the solution, every step shown; (d) the economic interpretation; (e) the
verification, by the Python check scripts and against the book's worked answer or the MWG solutions manual where one exists;
(f) common mistakes. Ten solutions (items 1, 2, 6, 8, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17, 21) were written for the earlier list
\texttt{lista-reais-lectures-01-05} and are input from
\texttt{../lista-reais-lectures-01-05-solutions/}; they are verified by \texttt{check\_A2.py} there. The twelve others are new and are
verified by \texttt{check\_L2.py} in this folder. Books: Tadelis (2013), \MG{} \& Toro-Gonzalez (2019, 2nd ed.),
Gibbons (1992), Mas-Colell, Whinston \& Green (1995) (MWG). In bimatrices player~1 picks rows and the first payoff is
player~1's; \underline{underlined} payoffs are best replies.}
